## 🎓 Key Concepts

### Reconnaissance vs. Surveillance

| Aspect | Surveillance (❌) | Reconnaissance (✅) |
|--------|------------------|-------------------|
| Observer | THEY watch YOU | YOU watch YOUR systems |
| Purpose | Control/Profit | Defense |
| Consent | None | Explicit |
| Data | Centralized | Local |
| Retention | Forever | Ephemeral |

### Genetic Threat Detection

**Via BearDog Lineage:**
- Family nodes = trusted (genetic ancestry verified)
- Stranger nodes = potential threat (unknown lineage)
- Cryptographic trust (not behavioral profiling)
- Transparent criteria (genetic ancestry chain)

### Defensive Posture

**What We Do:**
- ✅ Detect threats TO YOU
- ✅ Protect YOUR systems
- ✅ Defend YOUR sovereignty

**What We Don't Do:**
- ❌ Attack others
- ❌ Profile behavior
- ❌ Monitor content
